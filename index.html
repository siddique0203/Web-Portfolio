About Me

I am a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from AIUB and currently working as a Support & QA Intern at EWN Bangladesh Ltd. I am passionate about software quality assurance, problem-solving, and building reliable, user-focused software solutions.

I have hands-on experience in manual testing, test case design, bug reporting, smoke testing, API testing, and basic test automation. I am familiar with the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC), including requirements analysis, test planning, test execution, defect reporting, retesting, and regression testing.

My technical background includes C#, Java, Python, JavaScript, PHP, SQL, ASP.NET Core, HTML, and CSS. I also have experience working with tools and technologies such as Selenium WebDriver, Postman, GitHub, Jira, SQL Server, and MySQL.

I enjoy analyzing complex problems, identifying issues, and contributing to effective solutions. As a collaborative and detail-oriented individual, I value clear communication, proper documentation, continuous learning, and delivering high-quality software. My goal is to continue growing as a Software Quality Assurance Engineer while expanding my knowledge of software development, testing methodologies, and modern technologies.
